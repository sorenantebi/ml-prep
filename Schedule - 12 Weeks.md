## Rules

- **Coding pace:** every problem gets a fixed slot: **🟢 Easy 10 min, 🟡 Medium 30 min, 🔴 Hard 45 min**. If you have no approach after a third of the slot (3 / 10 / 15 min), open the Solution, read it, close it, and rewrite it from memory in the time left. When the slot ends, move on. If you did not solve it alone, it goes on the Fumbled list in [[STUDYPLAN]]. No 🔴 Hard problems are scheduled; swap one in for any two Mediums if you want to try one.
- **System design:** read only the Question note, design out loud on a blank Excalidraw board, then compare. Never read the Solution first.
- **AI fundamentals:** answer out loud before opening the collapsed answers.
- **Daytime LeetCode:** the problems under each date's *Before 19:00* line are extras for any free time before the evening session, using the same slot lengths. They come from the sections you are studying that week, then earlier ones, and are never the same as an evening problem. Tick them in their section notes like the others. Skip them on a heavy day rather than rushing, and add more if you have time.
- **Spaced review:** treat the review blocks as non-negotiable. They are what makes the first pass stick. If time is short, shorten new material, not review.
- **If you miss a session:** do not cram it in. Shift the rest of the day to the next free block, and drop the lowest-priority item (items marked optional first).
- **Total:** 131 NeetCode problems (51 🟢 Easy and 80 🟡 Medium, about 48 h of problem time, spread across all 18 sections) in the evening sessions, plus 96 (9 🟢, 75 🟡, 12 🔴) daytime problems (2 on each Mon–Thu), 22 system design problems + 2 unseen mocks, all 12 AI fundamentals topics, 10 coding challenges, 4 AI case studies and the Claude agent project (about 4.5 h plus spill-over).
